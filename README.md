## Project Overview
The Inventory Management System is a web-based application designed to help small businesses and organizations efficiently track, manage, and monitor their product inventory in real time. It provides a centralized platform for recording stock levels, managing item information, and generating inventory reports.

## Problem Statement
Small businesses and organizations often struggle to maintain accurate and up-to-date records of their inventory. Manual tracking through spreadsheets or paper logs leads to stock discrepancies, overstocking or understocking of items, and difficulty in generating timely reports. This system addresses these challenges by providing a digital, centralized, and structured inventory management solution.

## Target Users
- Warehouse staff and stockroom personnel
- Store managers and business owners
- Administrative staff responsible for procurement and supply chain

## Main Features
1. Add a new inventory item with details such as name, category, quantity, and unit price.
2. View all inventory items in a searchable and filterable list.
3. Update item details including quantity adjustments and price changes.
4. Delete inventory items that are no longer in use.
5. Monitor low-stock alerts when item quantities fall below a defined threshold.

## Proposed Technology Stack
- Frontend: Vue.js
- Backend: Node.js and Express
- Database: MongoDB Atlas Free
- Version Control: Git and GitHub
